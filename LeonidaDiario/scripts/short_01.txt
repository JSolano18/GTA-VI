Ignore Lucia in GTA 6 and THIS happens 💔 #gta6 #shorts

[1] DON'T IGNORE HER CALLS
If you keep ignoring Lucia's calls in GTA 6, you're going to lose a lot more than a conversation.

[2] THE RELATIONSHIP METER
Jason and Lucia have a relationship meter with four levels. Guides list them from Running on Empty, all the way up to Hot Together.

[3] WHAT RAISES IT
Holding hands, greeting each other, starting conversations, even going to bed at the same time. And both of them have to put in the effort.

[4] WHAT BREAKS IT
Neglect her, and the hand holding and kissing disappear until you rebuild the bond. Ignored calls and texts are one of the fastest ways to hurt it.

[5] IT CAN CHANGE THE STORY
Some guides even report that a broken relationship can change dialogue, mission options and cutscenes.

[6] NO JEALOUSY, THOUGH
The good news? There's no jealousy system. A strip club visit won't start a fight. So, are you a good partner in Leonida?
