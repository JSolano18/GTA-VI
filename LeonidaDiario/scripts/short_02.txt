You can make Jason FAT in GTA 6 🍔 (but not THAT fat) #gta6 #shorts

[1] GTA 6 IS WATCHING YOU EAT
GTA 6 is watching what you eat. And yes, you can actually make Jason fat.

[2] CONFIRMED BY ROCKSTAR
Rockstar confirmed it to Dazed. Jason and Lucia's bodies change with what they eat and how hard they train. Overeat and you gain weight. Hit the gym and you build muscle.

[3] THERE'S A HEALTH APP
There's even a health app tracking sleep, diet and weight. And if you stop training, those gains fade.

[4] "TRUE FAT JASON"
One player fed Jason burgers and tacos to build True Fat Jason. The result? A belly and a double chin, but players say he never gets as big as Arthur Morgan at his heaviest.

[5] AND IT SMELLS
And there's a smell system too. Rockstar's Aaron Garbut told Edge that Jason and Lucia react with disgust to bad smells across the map.

[6] GYM OR TACOS?
So, what's your Jason running on right now? Gym, or tacos?
